Jiggle Jar – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Jiggle Jar is a casual puzzle game. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the game.

What the game stores

Your best score, unlocked jellies, sound settings and reward status are saved on your own device only. We do not run servers that receive this data.

TikTok login

When played inside TikTok, the game uses TikTok's silent login as required by the platform. We do not store, sell or share your TikTok account information, and we do not receive your name, contacts, messages or location.

Advertising

The game offers optional rewarded video ads (for example to revive or get a power-up). Ads are served by the platform (TikTok, or the website hosting the game), which may collect data under its own privacy policy. Watching ads is always optional.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Clear the game's data or uninstall/remove the mini game to delete everything stored on your device.
